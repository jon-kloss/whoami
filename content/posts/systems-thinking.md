Most bugs aren't caused by a single line of code. They're caused by incorrect assumptions about how parts of a system interact.

## Boundaries matter

The most important architectural decision is where you draw the boundaries. Get the boundaries right and the implementation details become straightforward.

## Feedback loops

Every system has feedback loops. The question is whether you've designed them intentionally or they've emerged accidentally.

![System diagram](/images/system-diagram.png)

Understanding feedback loops helps you predict where a system will be stable and where it will oscillate.
