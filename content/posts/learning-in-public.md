I started writing about the things I was learning not because I had anything original to say, but because the act of writing forced me to understand things more deeply.

## The explanation test

If you can't explain something simply, you don't understand it well enough. Writing is the ultimate explanation test. Every fuzzy concept, every hand-wavy understanding gets exposed when you try to put it into words.

## It compounds

Each piece of writing becomes a reference point. Six months from now, when I encounter the same problem, I have my own notes to look back on. And occasionally, someone else finds them useful too.
